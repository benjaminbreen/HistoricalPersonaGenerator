A maker of fired clay and useful forms, living at the intersection of family expectation, seasonal country, and local exchange.

At forty-four, Tjandrawati has settled into work that is both practical and social. Pots move through the household; a good firing carries his name beyond it. His nearest concerns are weather, reliable materials, kin obligations, and the slow accumulation of respect.

He has a quiet public manner. Those who know him read steadiness rather than ambition in it, though he is attentive to the openings a marriage connection and a well-made object can create.
